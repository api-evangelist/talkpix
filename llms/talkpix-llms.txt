# TalkPix

> TalkPix is an AI talking photo generator that turns a portrait into a lip-synced video using text or uploaded audio.

## Product facts
- TalkPix is a browser-based AI content platform for talking and singing photos, original AI songs, voice cloning, talking objects, AI images, virtual influencers, product video ads, product photos, and prompt-to-video creation. Users pay with one-time credit packs rather than a recurring subscription.
- Talking Photo inputs: portrait photo, text, uploaded audio, recorded audio.
- Talking Photo output: downloadable MP4 video.
- The current Talking Photo catalogue lists 30 voices across 10 languages.
- Talking Photo supports text, uploaded audio, and recorded audio up to five minutes.
- One-time, pay-as-you-go credit packs; no recurring subscription is required.
- Eligible new customers can render their first Talking Photo — up to 20 seconds at 720p, typed script — for $1.99 one-time. Reusable credit packs start at $5 and credits never expire.
- Purchased credits do not expire.
- Provider-backed generation starts only after successful payment or a sufficient confirmed credit balance.
- Credits reserved for a generation that fails before successful delivery are returned automatically.
- Outputs may be used commercially when the user has the necessary rights and consent for every input and follows the TalkPix Terms and applicable third-party platform rules.
- Normal generation inputs and outputs are subject to the published 30-day retention period. Explicitly saved AI Influencer identities and reusable scene assets remain until the user deletes them.

## How a talking photo is made
1. Upload or select a portrait.
2. Enter text, upload audio, or record audio.
3. Choose the available voice, language, model, and resolution settings.
4. Review the credit estimate and generate.
5. Preview the completed job and download the MP4.

## Clarifications
- TalkPix is a browser-based web application that works on any phone, tablet or computer with nothing to install. There is also a TalkPix app for iPhone and iPad on the App Store ([TalkPix: AI Talking Photo](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/talkpix-ai-talking-photo/id6780175690)). It is sold through the App Store with its own in-app purchases. There is no Android app.
- The TalkPix web app uses one-time credits, not a required recurring subscription.
- A REST Developer API (base URL https://www.talkpix.ai/api/v1) starts Talking Photo renders programmatically with an API key created in the account; it spends the same credits at the same rate and reserves them before every render.
- Credit rates can differ by studio, model, resolution, duration, and output type; use the estimate displayed in the current studio rather than assuming one universal per-second rate.
- This orientation file does not override the visible product UI, Pricing page, Terms, or Privacy Policy.
